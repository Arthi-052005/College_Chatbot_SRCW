# Sri Ramakrishna College of Arts & Science for Women (SRCW), Coimbatore

Source: official website https://srcw.ac.in/ (pages: Home, Programmes Offered, Admission Regulations, Placement, Infrastructure). Information as published on the website in September/October 2026.

## About the college
- Full name: Sri Ramakrishna College of Arts & Science for Women (SRCW), also written SRCASW.
- Located in Siddhapudur, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. It is a women's college.
- Founded in 1991. Motto: "Educate to Empower". The college has an academic heritage of over 35 years.
- Affiliated to Bharathiar University. Reaccredited by NAAC with A+ Grade. An ISO certified institution.
- Conferred Autonomous status by UGC from 2024 onwards.
- Run by the SNR Sons Charitable Trust (website: https://www.snrsonscharitabletrust.org/). Dr. Sundar Ramakrishnan is the Managing Trustee.
- Principal: Dr. K. Chitra.
- Quality policy: committed to total quality in education, higher academic standards, building skills, fostering values and developing holistic personalities to empower women to meet global challenges.

## Admission and eligibility
- UG: candidates who have passed the Higher Secondary Examination conducted by the Government of Tamil Nadu, or an equivalent examination, are eligible for the first year of UG degree courses, subject to any specific conditions prescribed by Bharathiar University for each course.
- PG: candidates who have passed an undergraduate degree of Bharathiar University or an equivalent examination are eligible for the first year of PG courses, subject to any specific conditions prescribed by the University.
- Ph.D.: selection is based on merit, performance in the Common Entrance Test (CET) conducted by Bharathiar University, and an interview. Candidates who have cleared SET, UGC NET, UGC-CSIR-NET or GATE are exempted from CET.
- Original documents are verified on the date of admission.
- Foreign students need a valid student visa or provisional student visa and must obtain clearance from the Registrar, Bharathiar University.
- Bharathiar University eligibility criteria PDF: https://srcw.ac.in/themes/frontend/pdf/Eligibilty-condition-23-24.pdf
- Specific fee amounts and last dates are not listed on these pages; students should check https://srcw.ac.in/ or contact the college office.

## Differently abled students
Special care and attention is given. Ramp facilities, wheel-chair facilities, classrooms and restrooms are provided on the ground floor.

## Placement cell
- The Training & Placement Cell bridges academia and industry and arranges internships, training programmes and campus placements.
- It offers career guidance, resume-building and interview skills workshops, and industry interactions.
- Key recruiters are from IT, Banking, BPO, KPO, hospitals, research laboratories and pharmaceutical companies.
- Companies that have offered placements include TCS, Infosys BPO, Amazon, IDBI Federal LIC Co Ltd and IT World Corporate Office, among others.
- The college states that it closes final placements each year at about 98%.
- Placement 2022-2023: 404 students registered, 330 placed.

### Placement training by year
- First year: bridge courses and communication skills training.
- Second year: technical training and leadership enhancement training.
- Third year: aptitude training and soft skill training.

### Skill training programmes
- Soft skills: Employability Skill Training Programme, English Competency Development, Aptitude Competency Development.
- Language: ORELL Language Lab (self-learning programme for all students); STEP (Standardized Test for English Proficiency) for all first-year UG students.
- Life skills: Yoga, self-defence workshops, outbound training, health and hygiene programmes.
- ICT/computing skills: IIT Spoken Tutorial, Learnathan, Cloud Literacy, Digital Literacy.
